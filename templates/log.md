# Log

> Append-only. Newest at the bottom. One entry per ingest, lint, X-ray, watch run or filed answer.
>
> Format:
>
> ```
> ## [YYYY-MM-DD] <ingest|lint|xray|watch|ask|outcome> | <title>
> - Source: [[source-slug]]
> - Pages created: [[...]]
> - Pages updated: [[...]]
> - Contradictions: <none, or what disagreed and how it was resolved>
> - Open follow-ups: <what the user still needs to do>
> ```
