# Person

## Steps

1. Find their page in `People/` through `index.md`. If none exists, offer to create one from what the user knows, plus a public web search for their current title (public pages only).
2. Read the page, their company page, the application, and the two or three most recent sources that mention them.
3. Answer in this order:
   - **Where things stand:** the last interaction, the stage, and what happens next.
   - **Open loops, split by who is blocked:** what the user owes them, what they owe the user. Oldest first, with dates.
   - **What they care about:** in their own words where the sources have them.
   - **For the next conversation:** 2-3 things to say or ask, drawn from the above and from the company's interview angles.
4. If anything is overdue, offer to draft the follow-up email. Draft only; never send.
